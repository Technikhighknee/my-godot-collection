**Hey, my name is Jason!**

I'm currently working on a medieval economy / dynasty / life sim game inspired by The Guild 2, using Godot Engine.

This is a passion project of mine. Something I genuinely want to build, and something I'm willing to invest a significant amount of my time and energy into.

My goal? **Eventually release it on Steam.**

I've spent quite some time working on the technical foundation, building systems for character selection, movement, actions, buildings, interiors, inventories, production, ownership, dynasties and more.

Now I want to bring all of this together into an actual playable prototype. And while doing that, figure out what works, what doesn't, and gradually refine the systems.

**And that's where I'm looking for someone to join me!**

I'm handling the programming and game design, but I'd love to find a 3D artist who wants to help shape the visual identity of the game.

### What would we build first?

The prototype would focus on a small medieval town and some of its core gameplay.

For the visual side, we'd need roughly:

- A place for workers to sleep (no interior)
- A tavern with two interiors (dining hall and brewing cellar)
- A market / market area
- A small hut / starter house with an interior
- A town hall with an interior
- A small map and environment
- Roads, terrain and ground textures
- A shared set of props and furniture
- A simple character model with a few clothing color variations
- Some basic UI icons

It might sound like quite a bit, but we're not aiming for highly detailed assets right away.

Buildings can share textures, materials and props. Interiors would be simple, camera-friendly spaces rather than complex floor plans.

I'd much rather start with something simple and charming, and gradually increase the level of detail as the game grows.

**What matters is that the prototype already starts to feel like its own little world.**

### What kind of collaboration am I looking for?

I'm not looking for someone who just follows instructions and produces assets from a checklist.

Quite the opposite.

If you think a building should look completely different from what I imagined, I want to hear it!

You're the artist. Your experience, ideas and creativity should have a real influence on the game.

And while we'll naturally have our own areas of expertise, I don't believe in the whole "that's your responsibility, not mine" attitude.

We're building something together. If you need a hand and I can help, I will.

### Where do I want to take this?

The prototype is only the beginning.

I'd like to use it to establish the game's visual identity, refine the gameplay and eventually bring more people on board, especially someone for music and sound design.

I'm serious about wanting to make this game, but I'm not expecting anyone to treat an unpaid project like a full-time job.

Which brings me to something important:

**There is no upfront funding right now. This is a revenue-sharing collaboration.**

I can't promise the game will make money. But if you help build it, you deserve a fair share of what it earns.

That's not negotiable as a principle. The actual terms, of course, are something we'd work out together.

### And one last thing...

I'm not just looking for someone with the right skills.

I'd love to find someone who shares that feeling of wanting to create something.

Someone who gets excited about building things, experimenting, solving problems, and watching an idea slowly turn into something real.

Maybe this becomes more than just a collaboration. Maybe even a friendship.

I think that would be pretty cool.
